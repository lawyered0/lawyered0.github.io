# LawyeredOS public beta

**Claude with the right legal-work skills—not another legal SaaS dashboard.**

LawyeredOS works with the matter files you choose, keeps recorded history and
state in your workspace, and helps identify and execute the next step under
lawyer oversight.

Download the **[macOS 0.2.0 beta.2 source ZIP](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lawyered0/lawyered0.github.io/main/downloads/lawyeredos-0.2.0-macos-beta.2.zip)**.

```text
SHA-256: 0f3eccf6e62e0bcb7ffbb88a6c14dd2f96c8a05acba73b93bb4bff3173af7cd5
```

Extract the ZIP, open its `lawyeredos` folder in the Claude desktop app's
**Code** tab, and choose **Local**. This beta is macOS-only; Python 3.11 or newer
is required, and Python 3.12 is the fully tested runtime.

Setup now asks explicitly whether to connect Gmail through Claude, skip email,
or use the experimental local read-only adapter. It does not silently leave
email out, store credentials, or enable automatic sending.

LawyeredOS is software, not legal advice. Verify the work before relying,
sending, signing, filing, or serving. Local files are not the same as offline
processing: selected content may be sent to Anthropic under your Claude account,
settings, and terms.

The complete website and beta download are published from this repository. The
development repository remains private.
